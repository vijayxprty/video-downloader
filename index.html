Only download your own videos, public-domain / Creative Commons videos, or videos you have permission for.

Backend settings

Locally this is http://localhost:8000. When deployed, set it to your free Hugging Face Space URL. No private URL is hard-coded into this page.

Private, login-only, removed, age-restricted, geo-blocked, or DRM-protected videos are not downloaded. You will get a specific reason why, not a generic error. Downloaded files are not stored permanently — the backend deletes its temporary copy after sending it to you.
